🚦 Traffic Jams vs Factories: Which Causes More Pollution?

📌 About the Project

I had a simple question in my mind:

“Does traffic pollution have a bigger impact than factories?”

We see traffic jams almost every day, and we also know that factories release a lot of pollutants. So I wanted to compare both using data instead of just making assumptions.

For this project, I worked with pollution, vehicle/traffic and industrial data from India and tried to understand how they are related to air quality.

🎯 What I Wanted to Find Out

- 🚗 How much traffic and vehicles are related to pollution
- 🏭 How industrial activity is related to pollution
- 🌫️ How AQI, PM2.5 and PM10 change over time
- 📍 Which locations show higher pollution levels
- 📊 Whether traffic or factories show a stronger relationship with air pollution

🛠️ Tools Used

Microsoft Excel

- Data Cleaning
- Pivot Tables
- Data Analysis
- Charts & Visualizations
- KPI Analysis
- Dashboard
- Trend Analysis

📊 Dashboard

I created an Excel dashboard to make the comparison easier to understand.

It includes different charts, KPIs and trends related to air quality, traffic/vehicles and industrial activity.

💡 What I Learned

This project helped me get more comfortable with real-world data.

I learned that simply looking at numbers isn't enough. You have to clean the data, find patterns, compare different factors and then try to understand what those patterns actually mean.

The main goal was simple:

Let the data tell the story. 📈
